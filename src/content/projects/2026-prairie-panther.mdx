Prairie Panther is the 2025–2026 iteration of UMRT's rover platform. Its pink
equipment enclosure sits between widely spaced wheel assemblies with exposed
linkages and coil springs; the arm and communications masts extend above it.

Alongside the physical rover, the season's published engineering work developed
joint-trajectory arm control, CAN-connected servo hardware, and an RS485 soil
sensor interface. Mission Control continued the science-task and underbody
sampling-arm work introduced in the previous season.
