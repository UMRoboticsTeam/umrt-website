Prairie Pioneer was UMRT's first competition platform for CIRC 2024. Its
four-wheel extrusion frame supported a white equipment enclosure, an elevated
robotic arm, and a communications mast.

The first-year design established the rover's ROS 2 drive, camera, GPS, and
Arduino arm-control foundations. Payload handled the season's science and
task-specific equipment; Mission Control joined the team structure in the
following year.
