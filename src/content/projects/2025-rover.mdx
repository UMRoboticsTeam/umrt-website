Project Perry brought an open teal chassis and a blue geared arm to the
2024–2025 season. The software moved the drivetrain to CAN commands and
documented the arm's differential wrist, servo gripper, and planning tools.

The season also separated camera traffic from control and sensor telemetry.
Mission Control replaced the first-year Payload section, combining science-task
preparation with development of a servo-actuated sampling arm under the chassis.
